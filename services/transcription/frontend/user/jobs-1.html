Test Assignment: Short Speech Transcription

Practice task · Browser audio sample · Test submission only

TEST ONLY

This is a single test assignment for checking the jobs page, section toggles, browser audio playback, and submission validation.

  • Task type: short speech transcription
  • Source: generated speech played by your browser
  • Submission mode: local interface test
  • Payment status: test task, not payable

Listen to the complete sample before typing. Transcribe the spoken words as accurately as possible. Replay the sample as needed, then compare your text with what you heard.

  • Do not summarize or rewrite the speaker's meaning.
  • Keep the original word order.
  • Use punctuation that makes the speech easy to read.
  • Do not add information that was not spoken.

The browser will read the sample aloud using its built-in speech synthesis. Audio availability and voice quality depend on the device and browser.

Sample speech

Good morning, everyone. Please review the three items on the agenda. First, we will discuss the project budget. Next, we will check the delivery schedule. Finally, we will agree on the tasks for the coming week.

  • Use standard English spelling.
  • Include spoken words rather than sound descriptions.
  • Use commas and full stops where appropriate.
  • Do not invent speaker labels when only one speaker is heard.
  • If you cannot hear a word clearly, replay the sample before deciding.
  • Submit one readable paragraph.
  • Use normal sentence capitalization.
  • Keep punctuation consistent.
  • Do not include a title or commentary in the transcript.

Before testing submission, check each item:

  • Every spoken sentence is included.
  • The agenda items remain in the correct order.
  • Spelling and punctuation are readable.
  • No extra explanation has been added.
  • The text box contains your finished transcript.
